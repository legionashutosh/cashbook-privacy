Privacy Policy for Cash Book - Expense & Budget

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Cash Book ("the app") is a personal finance utility that helps you record cash inflows, cash outflows, expenses, budgets, and financial reports. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, including its advertising and analytics services.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter financial records and preferences such as transaction amounts, notes, dates, times, budget details, currency preferences, and receipt or photo attachments. These details are entered by you to use the app's features.

2. Local Storage and Financial Records

The app is designed to store the financial records you enter locally on your device for its core functionality. The app does not require you to create an online account to use these core features, and this policy does not describe a Cash Book-operated cloud account or financial-record server.

Financial records stored locally may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, reset your device, or otherwise remove the app's data. You are responsible for keeping any exports or backups you choose to create. Files you export or share may be handled by the destination application or service you select.

3. Photos, Camera, and Receipt Attachments

If you choose to attach a receipt or photo, the app may use the relevant media picker, camera, or device permission. The selected image is used for the related app feature. Avoid attaching information you do not want stored on your device or included in an export.

4. Reports and Exports

If you use report or export features, the app processes the selected financial information to create the requested file. Exported files are saved or shared according to your device settings and the destination you choose.

5. Google AdMob Advertising

Cash Book uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your settings, location, consent choices, and applicable law, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising IDs or other device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, ad requests, ad impressions, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

The app's advertising SDK may report impression-level information, including the ad format, ad source, and estimated impression revenue, for advertising measurement and app monetization analytics. Revenue measurement information is not the same as the financial transactions you enter into Cash Book.

Advertising behavior can vary by region, device settings, and the consent choices available to you. Google may process information under its own terms and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

6. Firebase Analytics and Usage Measurement

The app uses Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics to understand app usage, troubleshoot the experience, and measure advertising performance. Depending on the installed app version and configuration, events may include first app open, sessions, screen views, ad requests, ad impressions, and advertising revenue measurement. These events can include event timestamps, app and device information, and relevant technical identifiers. Advertising revenue events may include an estimated revenue value, currency, ad format, and ad source.

Analytics data may be linked to the app's Google Analytics property and, where configured, shared with linked Google advertising services for measurement and campaign optimization. Analytics and advertising identifiers and processing may be subject to your consent choices, device settings, regional requirements, and Google's policies. The app does not intend to send the transaction amounts, notes, or other personal finance entries you type into its local records as Analytics event parameters.

Learn more about Firebase privacy and security and Google Analytics data safeguards.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the app uses Google's consent tools to present applicable consent information before requesting ads or using features that require consent. The choices and messages shown can vary according to your region, app configuration, and the applicable requirements. You should make your choice based on the information displayed in the consent form.

If a privacy-options or consent-settings control is available in the app, you can use it to review or change the choices supported by the current version. You can also review your device's advertising and privacy settings. Changing a device setting may not erase data already processed by a third-party provider.

The presence of a consent SDK does not mean every processing activity requires consent in every region, nor does it guarantee that a consent flow is configured correctly in every app release. The app's behavior depends on the installed version and its live configuration.

8. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and the installed version, the app may request access related to notifications, camera, photos or media, biometric authentication, or saving and sharing exported files. Permissions are used for their relevant functionality and can generally be managed through Android settings.

9. Security

The app may offer device-level or app-level security features such as PIN, pattern, or biometric authentication where supported by the installed version and device. No method of electronic storage or authentication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The app uses Google AdMob and Firebase / Google Analytics for advertising, analytics, and related technical services. Information processed by these providers may be handled on their systems and shared as described in their own policies and according to the app's configuration, your choices, and applicable law. The app's local financial records are intended for the app's core on-device features and are not intentionally sent as analytics event parameters.

11. Children's Privacy

Cash Book is a general-purpose finance utility and is not specifically directed toward children. The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records and attachments stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or take another action that removes them. Analytics and advertising information processed by Google or other third-party providers is retained and deleted according to the applicable service settings, provider policies, and legal requirements. Removing local app data does not necessarily delete information already processed by a third-party service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or Cash Book, use the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.
